# Interface direction

L4 uses an achromatic palette, a four-L mark, bundled Geist under SIL OFL 1.1, small corner radii and clear typography. Preserve the current composition while fixing observed layout and interaction defects.

The landing explains communities, shows the interface, and offers Cloudflare or experimental VPS deployment. The Shell groups Servers by Instance and embeds the Instance's own Client. Accounts and content remain local to that Instance. Native iOS/macOS packaging is frozen.

Inherited product screenshots are sample content and must be disclosed until replaced by current L4 captures. The optional sandbox link may open a sign-in form; it is not an anonymous read-only demo. Do not claim current browser/device acceptance from prototype critique scores or archived screenshots.

Review the first community journey, empty states, navigation, composer and settings at phone and desktop widths as described in the roadmap. Record actual evidence for the new source instead of copying prototype design scores.
